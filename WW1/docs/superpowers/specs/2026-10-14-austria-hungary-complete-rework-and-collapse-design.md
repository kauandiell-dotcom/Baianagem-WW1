## 1. Intent and The Why

The Austro-Hungarian Monarchy (`AUS`) is the tragic catalyst and eastern pillar of the Central Powers in World War I. In *Baianagem-WW1*, forensic analysis reveals that its existing content suffers from severe systemic defects:
1. **Procedural "Ghost" Focus Tree:** The existing 200-focus file (`common/national_focus/austria.txt`) was automatically generated by an external script (`scripts/focus_layout.py`). Almost all focuses are sterile copy-pastes granting generic +40 PP, 25 XP, or setting empty country flags (`auh_ww1_...`).
2. **Purely Cosmetic Constitutional Paths:** Focuses such as *Trialism* and *Danubian Federation* merely change the country's cosmetic name and flag, offering zero mechanical divergence, no unique autonomy/vassal systems, no Hungarian backlash, and no real gameplay impact.
3. **Absence of Historical Wartime Drama:** Crucial historical pillars—the Vienna Bread Crisis (*Brot des Kaisers*), the Babel of 11 regimental languages, the Conrad von Hötzendorf vs. Svetozar Boroević military rivalry, Škoda 30.5 cm siege artillery, the Colonel Redl 1913 espionage scandal, and the Sixtus Affair separate peace negotiations—are entirely absent.
4. **No Dynamic Collapse Mechanics:** In 1918, the real Austro-Hungarian Empire disintegrated under the weight of catastrophic casualties, famine, and ethnic secession. The mod currently has no historical collapse or fragmentation engine; when Austria loses, it collapses abruptly without player agency.

### Core Architecture & Anti-Bug Guarantees
Learning from previous rework edge-cases (e.g. Day 1 Versailles or Day 1 Russian civil wars), this rework enforces **strict fail-safe principles**:
- **Zero Day 1 Premature Crises:** All collapse and crisis triggers are strictly gated behind `has_war = yes`, legitimate wartime threshold dates (`date > 1915.1.1`+), verified core state controls (`controls_state = 4` for Vienna), and high surrender progress (`> 0.40`).
- **Tag-Safe Scripted Internal Wars (No Dynamic Clones or Faction Chaos):** Breakaway tags use fixed Clausewitz tags (`CZE`, `HUN`, `CRO`). They receive strict `set_rule = { can_join_factions = no }` and AI strategies, preventing them from joining the Entente or dragging foreign powers into local rebellions.
- **Audited Semantic Modifiers:** 100% of national ideas, spirits, and focus rewards use verified Clausewitz modifiers from `data/hoi4_valid_country_modifiers.json`. Zero broken or arbitrary variables.

## 2. Goals, Non-Goals, and Anti-Goals

### Goals
- **G1: The 4 Divergent Constitutional Destinies:** Four deeply differentiated political trajectories:
  1. *Dualism of Iron (Viena-Budapest Pact / István Tisza)*: Centralized military might, 100% Honvéd manpower, Hungarian food security, but high Czech and South Slavic dissent.
  2. *Habsburg Trialism (Franz Ferdinand's Dream)*: Creates the Third Crown of Illyria in Zagreb, pacifies the South Slavs, and neutralizes Serbian revanchism, triggering the Budapest Diet Crisis.
  3. *United States of Greater Austria / Danubian Federation (Popovici & Karl I)*: Decentralized autonomous crownland states with massive internal economic synergy and zero separatist revolt risk, but consensus-bound war decisions.
  4. *Military Junker Dictatorship (AOK / Conrad von Hötzendorf)*: Dissolution of parliaments, war surveillance office (*Kriegsüberwachungsamt*), summary execution of deserters, extreme offensive shock attack, but risk of communist workers' revolution.
- **G2: The Crown Dashboard & Imperial Weariness Engine:** A dynamic 3-vector simulation tracking:
  - *Imperial War Weariness (0–100%)*
  - *Nationalities Cohesion (0–100%)*
  - *Vienna Grain Stockpile (0–100)*
- **G3: Dynamic, Non-Fatal Fragmentation Mechanics:** If weariness exceeds 75% and critical fronts collapse, the Empire faces path-dependent secession wars that the player can contain, defeat, and reunify through military victory.
- **G4: Historical Campaign Arc (1911–1923):** Pre-war espionage (Colonel Redl Affair, Balkan Crisis), three-front war (Galicia, Serbia, Alpine Isonzo front), succession of Karl I, Sixtus peace negotiations, and post-war Danubian hegemony.
- **G5: Single UTF-8 BOM & Bilingual Parity:** 100% English and Brazilian Portuguese (`l_braz_por`) localization with zero double-BOM corruption.
- **G6: Automated Verification:** 100% passing rate in `tests/test_ww1_austria_hungary.py` integrated into `scripts/qa_audit_all.py`.

### Non-Goals
- **NG1:** We are NOT transforming Austria-Hungary into WW2-era Nazi Ostmark. Content is strictly contained to the Great War era (1911–1923).
- **NG2:** We are NOT using dynamic `start_civil_war` commands; all conflicts use tag-safe scripted spawns.

### Anti-Goals
- **AG1 (The Day 1 Collapse Bug):** Rebellions or capitulation events firing before war or under peaceful conditions.
- **AG2 (The Entente World War Cascade):** Internal Hungarian or Czech rebels joining the Entente faction and breaking global war diplomacy.
- **AG3 (The Condemned Monarchy):** Scripted unavoidable collapse. A skilled player who manages grain, wins on the battlefield, and balances ethnic diets can survive, prosper, and achieve total victory.

## 4. Architectural Pillars of the Rework

### Pillar 1: The Crown Dashboard & The 3 Survival Vectors
Tracked via variables updated by weekly on-actions and tactical events:

1. **Imperial War Weariness (`AUH_war_weariness`):**
   - Scaled by cumulative combat casualties, enemy occupation of core states (Vienna 4, Budapest 43, Bohemia 9, Galicia 88), and duration of war.
   - Reduced by major victories (Belgrade taken, fall of Venice, Brest-Litovsk peace with Russia).
2. **Nationalities Cohesion (`AUH_cohesion`):**
   - Represents imperial legitimacy among Czechs, Poles, South Slavs, and Romanians.
   - Managed via Diet decisions, language reforms, and constitutional choices.
3. **Vienna Grain Stockpile (`AUH_vienna_grain`):**
   - Represents food supply in Cisleithanian urban centers.
   - Depleted if Hungary withholds grain; replenished via Romanian oil/grain agreements, Ukrainian bread peace, or domestic requisitioning.

### Pillar 2: The 4 Divergent Constitutional Paths

#### Branch A: Dualism of Iron (Tisza's Magiar Supremacy)
- **Concept:** Reaffirm the 1867 Ausgleich. Total alliance with Hungarian Prime Minister István Tisza.
- **Mechanics:**
  - Hungarian grain flow guaranteed (+30 Grain, zero bread riots).
  - Unlocks elite *Honvéd* shock infantry and Magyar Hussars (+15% cavalry breakthrough).
  - Slavic provinces gain high unrest; Czech regiments risk mutiny on the Eastern Front.
  - If collapse triggers: Czechs (`CZE`) and South Slavs (`CRO`) revolt, but Austria and Hungary form the unbroken *Double Fortress*.

#### Branch B: Habsburg Trialism (The Crown of Illyria)
- **Concept:** Franz Ferdinand's plan to elevate Croats, Slovenes, and Bosnians into a third sovereign crown based in Zagreb.
- **Mechanics:**
  - Pacifies the South Slavs. Serbia loses its pan-Slavic liberation appeal (-30% Serbian volunteer rate).
  - Grants Svetozar Boroević elite mountain defense modifiers on the Italian front (+25% mountain defense).
  - **The Budapest Ultimatum:** Hungary furiously rejects the loss of Croatia and threatens armed secession. The player must either compromise via financial concessions or militarily disarm the Hungarian opposition.
  - If collapse triggers: Hungary (`HUN`) secedes, while Austria and Illyria fight side-by-side to preserve the realm.

#### Branch C: United States of Greater Austria / Danubian Mega-Federation (Popovici & Karl I)
- **Concept:** Transform the empire into a true multi-nation sovereign Mega-Federation on the map under Emperor Karl I, granting national autonomy to the historical crownlands while preserving extreme imperial centralization.
- **Constituent Federal Tags on Map:**
  - `HUN` (Kingdom of Hungary)
  - `CZE` (Kingdom of Bohemia & Moravia)
  - `CRO` (Kingdom of Croatia-Slavonia & Bosnia / Illyria)
  - `POL` (Kingdom of Galicia & Lodomeria / Austro-Poland)
- **Visual Color Matching (No Ugly Patchwork):**
  - All federal member states receive dynamic cosmetic tags (e.g. `HUN_danubian_federal`, `CZE_danubian_federal`, `CRO_danubian_federal`, `POL_danubian_federal`) that share Austria's exact imperial map color (`rgb { 215 195 160 }`). The realm appears united and elegant on the political map.
- **Extreme Centralization Mechanics (Never Weakens Overlord or AI):**
  - Subject states receive custom autonomy / national spirit `AUH_danubian_federal_member`:
    - +75% Civilian & Military factory output transferred directly to Vienna (`extra_trade_to_overlord_factor`, master building permissions, factory siphoning).
    - Overlord draws up to 70% subject manpower pool for imperial armies.
    - Federal states retain 25% of factories and adequate manpower to independently recruit and maintain their own territorial armies.
- **Initial Federal Division Spawns (4 to 18 Divisions Each):**
  - Upon emergence of the federation, each constituent state spawns historical territorial defense forces:
    - Hungary (`HUN`): 14 to 18 divisions (Royal Hungarian Honvéd).
    - Bohemia (`CZE`): 8 to 12 divisions (Bohemian Territorial Corps).
    - Croatia/Illyria (`CRO`): 4 to 8 divisions (Illyrian Border & Mountain Regiments).
    - Galicia (`POL`): 6 to 10 divisions (Galician Legion & Rifles).
- **Imperial Immunity:**
  - Secession risk is permanently eliminated (0%). The Empire gains unprecedented industrial synergy and defensive depth without losing manpower or economic throughput.

#### Branch D: Military Dictatorship (Conrad's AOK)
- **Concept:** Franz Conrad von Hötzendorf suspends civil liberties, establishes the War Surveillance Office (*Kriegsüberwachungsamt*), and rules via martial law.
- **Mechanics:**
  - Total militarization of Škoda and Steyr-Mannlicher (+30% military factory construction speed).
  - Deserters face immediate drumhead court-martial.
  - Extreme offensive bonus (+15% breakthrough, +10% division attack), but plummeting stability and high risk of socialist revolution in Vienna and Budapest.
  - If collapse triggers: Red Guards and sailor mutineers from Cattaro proclaim the Soviet Republic of Austria and Hungary.

### Pillar 5: Tag-Safe Scripted Fragmentation & Pacification Engine

#### The Fragmentation Trigger (Strictly Gated):
```clausewitz
trigger = {
    tag = AUS
    has_war = yes
    check_variable = { AUH_war_weariness > 75 }
    check_variable = { AUH_cohesion < 30 }
    OR = {
        surrender_progress > 0.40
        NOT = { controls_state = 4 } # Vienna
        NOT = { controls_state = 43 } # Budapest
        NOT = { controls_state = 88 } # Galicia
    }
}
```

#### Tag-Safe Breakaway Execution:
1. **Czech Secession (`CZE`):**
   - Transfers Bohemia (states 9, 69) to `CZE`.
   - Spawns defensive Czechoslovak Legion units in Prague.
   - Executes:
     ```clausewitz
     CZE = {
         set_rule = { can_join_factions = no }
         declare_war_on = { target = AUS type = civil_war }
     }
     ```
2. **Hungarian Secession (`HUN`) (Under Trialism):**
   - Transfers Hungarian core states to `HUN`.
   - Spawns Royal Hungarian Army units in Budapest.
   - Executes:
     ```clausewitz
     HUN = {
         set_rule = { can_join_factions = no }
         declare_war_on = { target = AUS type = civil_war }
     }
     ```
3. **South Slav Secession (`CRO`) (Under Dualism/Dictatorship):**
   - Transfers Croatia/Bosnia to `CRO`.
   - Enforces `can_join_factions = no`.

#### Imperial Reunification (Fast Clean Annexation):
- When `AUS` captures the rebel capital (Prague 9, Budapest 43, or Zagreb 109):
  - Event fires immediately: *Re-establishment of Imperial Authority*.
  - Rebel tag is annexed back cleanly.
  - Conspirators are tried, stability is restored (+20%), and zero broken peace conferences occur.
